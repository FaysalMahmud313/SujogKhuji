What SujogKhuji is

SujogKhuji is a free tool that collects links to jobs, internships, scholarships, competitions and programmes from the public web, and lets you track your own applications. We are not an employer, a recruiter or an agent for any organisation listed.

Listings

  • Every listing links to the page it came from. Details such as deadlines, pay, funding and experience are read automatically and can be wrong or out of date. Always confirm them on the original page before you apply.
  • A "Verified source" badge means the page is on a site we recognise. It is not a promise about the opportunity itself.
  • We never charge you, and real employers and scholarships do not ask for money to apply. Do not send money to anyone because of a listing here.
