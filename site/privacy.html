Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

SujogKhuji keeps as little about you as it can. Without an account, nothing about you leaves your browser. With an account, we store your email, your profile and the applications you track, so they appear on all your devices. We do not sell data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

What we store

  • Without an account: your profile, tracked applications and settings are saved only in this browser. Clearing the browser data removes them.
  • With an account: your email address, the profile you fill in (experience, name, field, institution, study status, graduation year and preferences), your tracked applications with their steps and notes, your saved searches, and any listings you report.
  • Sign-in emails: to send a verification code we use your email address once per code. To stop abuse we keep a scrambled fingerprint of the address for two days. It cannot be turned back into your email.

What we do not store

  • Your password. It is kept only as a one-way hash by our sign-in provider.
  • Your browsing on other sites, your location or your contacts.

Add from text

Without an account, text you paste is read inside your browser and never uploaded. When you are signed in, the text, link or name you enter is sent to our server, which may search the web for it and asks a free AI service to read it. The result is kept for up to 7 days so the same request is not processed twice. Free AI services may use what they receive to improve their models, so do not paste personal information such as your phone number or national ID.

Who helps us run the site

  • Supabase stores accounts and the database.
  • Our hosting provider serves the website and sends the verification emails through our mailbox.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, when switched on, checks that sign-ups come from people and not bots.
  • Web search services (Tavily, Serper and Exa) find and read opportunity pages. They receive search words and the addresses of public pages, never your account details.
  • Free AI services (Groq, Google Gemini and OpenRouter, whichever is available) read announcements and pages to pull out details, and look things up when you search by name. They receive the announcement text or the words you typed, never your account details.
  • Google Forms keeps the feedback you send. When you use the Feedback page, the two texts you wrote and your account email address are saved in a Google Form. Only the person who runs SujogKhuji can open it. We use it to fix problems and decide what to build next. Your email is added automatically from your sign-in, so that we can reply if needed.

Listings are found by an automatic web search and link to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy rules.

On your device

The app saves its data in your browser's storage so it works offline. If you turn off "Keep me signed in", your sign-in ends when you close the browser. Always sign out on a shared computer.

Your choices

  • See and change your data in Profile & settings.
  • Download a copy with Export.
  • Remove it from this browser with Clear everything.
  • To delete your account and everything stored with it, email the site owner from the address on your account.

Staying safe

Listings come from the open web. We filter out pages that ask applicants for money and mark sources we recognise, but we cannot check every page. Never pay to apply for a job or scholarship, and report anything suspicious from the listing's details.
